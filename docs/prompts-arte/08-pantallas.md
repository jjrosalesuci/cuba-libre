# 8. Pantallas y arte promocional

## 8.1 Pantalla de título (320×180)

El título en texto lo pone el juego; la imagen va **sin letras**.

```
[ESTILO de fondos]
Title screen background for a retro platformer, 16:9: a pixel-art map of the island of Cuba
glowing softly at night, seen from above, dark blue sea with stars. A thin red glowing path runs
along the island from the eastern tip to Havana in the west. In the lower left corner, a dark hill
with the silhouette of a man raising a wooden stick, against a sunrise glow at the bottom of the
image. Leave the top quarter mostly empty for the title text. No text.
```

## 8.2 Mapa entre niveles (320×180)

```
[ESTILO de fondos]
Top-down pixel-art map of the island of Cuba, dark green land with a light green coastline,
on a dark navy night sea with faint stars. Simple and clean, no city names, no text, no markers.
The island should fill the middle of the image from left to right. 16:9.
```

## 8.3 Final (para la fase 3)

```
[ESTILO de fondos]
Sunrise over Havana seen from the Malecón seawall: the city lights turning on everywhere,
people dancing in the streets with Cuban flags, the hero sitting on the seawall with his
flip-flops off, drinking a tiny cup of coffee. Warm, happy, celebratory. 16:9. No text.
```

## 8.4 Portada / póster (para itch.io, redes, etc.)

```
16-bit pixel art game cover in SNES box-art style, vertical 3:4.
A Cuban mulato man with curly hair, white tank top, blue shorts, yellow flip-flops and a wooden
stick leaps forward heroically. Behind him, a crowd of neighbors banging pots and waving Cuban flags,
a road winding across the island of Cuba from east to west toward Havana at sunrise.
Comedic cartoon tone, bright colors, no blood. Leave space at the top for a logo. No text.
```
